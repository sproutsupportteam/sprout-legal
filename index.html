Sprout - Nutrition Tracker

Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated August 11, 2026

Sprout - Nutrition Tracker ("Sprout," "we," "us," or "our") is operated by Ali Alavi. This Privacy Policy explains how Sprout collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the Sprout mobile application and related services.

Contact: sproutsupportteam@gmail.com

Educational use: Sprout provides educational nutrition information. It is not a medical service and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to Sprout's mobile application, account and guest features, food and nutrition tracking, meal-photo recognition, AI-assisted classification, notifications, support communications, and related backend services.

2. Information We Collect

Account and profile information

If you create an account, we may collect your email address, account identifier, display name, authentication information managed by our authentication provider, and profile settings. Depending on the features you use, profile settings may include age, gender selection, dietary restrictions, disliked foods, preferred cuisines, theme settings, and notification preferences.

We do not receive or store your plaintext account password.

Food, nutrition, and health-related information

We collect information that you choose to log, including food names, serving and portion information, food groups, calories, dates and times, nutrition goals, dietary preferences, notes, and classifications or insights generated by the app. Because this information relates to diet and nutrition, it may be considered health-related information in some jurisdictions.

Meal photos and other user content

If you choose photo recognition, Sprout processes the image you select or capture so that foods can be identified. A meal photo may be transmitted through our service providers for AI analysis and may be saved with the corresponding food-log entry. Notes and other text you enter are also user content.

Camera and photo-library access is optional and requested only when you use a feature that needs it. You can change these permissions in your device settings.

Guest information

You may use parts of Sprout without creating an account. We assign a random guest-session identifier and may store food logs associated with that identifier. A guest identifier is pseudonymous, but we treat it as personal information while it can be connected to a session or device.

Technical and operational information

Our service providers may automatically process limited network and technical information, such as IP address, request time, app or device information, error details, and security signals, to deliver the service, prevent abuse, diagnose problems, and protect accounts. Sprout does not use this information for advertising or cross-app tracking.

Support communications

If you contact us, we receive the email address and information you include in your message. Please do not send meal photos, medical records, or other sensitive information unless it is necessary for your request.

3. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Create and authenticate accounts.
  • Save and display food logs, preferences, and nutrition history.
  • Analyze meal photos and provide food-identification results.
  • Generate nutrition summaries, trends, classifications, and educational insights.
  • Personalize goals and recommendations selected by the user.
  • Schedule optional local notifications.
  • Provide support and respond to privacy requests.
  • Secure, maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the service.
  • Measure service usage using aggregated or minimized information.
  • Comply with law and enforce our terms and safety requirements.

We do not use Sprout data for third-party advertising.

4. AI Processing

When you use AI-assisted food classification or meal-photo recognition, relevant text, food information, and/or a selected meal photo is sent through our AWS-hosted service to Anthropic's commercial API. Anthropic processes this content to generate the requested result.

According to Anthropic's published commercial API practices as of this Policy's effective date:

  • Anthropic does not use commercial API inputs or outputs to train its models by default.
  • Standard API inputs and outputs are automatically deleted from Anthropic's backend within 30 days, subject to exceptions for services with different retention, contractual arrangements, legal requirements, and enforcement of Anthropic's Usage Policy.
  • Anthropic does not support ad hoc deletion of individual paid-API requests.
  • If Anthropic's automated safety systems flag content as a potential Usage Policy violation, Anthropic states that it may retain the relevant inputs and outputs for up to two years and related trust-and-safety classification scores for up to seven years. Anthropic may also retain data as required by law.

Anthropic's practices are governed by its own terms and may change. See Anthropic's commercial data-retention information and model-training information.

AI output can be inaccurate, incomplete, or misleading. Sprout's AI features are for educational assistance and should not be treated as medical advice.

Sprout:

  • Does not display third-party advertising.
  • Does not sell or rent personal information.
  • Does not share personal information with data brokers.
  • Does not use personal information for cross-app or cross-site tracking.
  • Does not send promotional marketing email.

If these practices change, we will update this Policy and provide any legally required notice or consent before using information for a materially different purpose.

7. Data Retention

Signed-in users

  • Raw daily food-log records, including associated meal photos, notes, food names, and exact timestamps, may be retained for up to 60 days, unless you delete them sooner.
  • After 60 days, raw daily records are deleted and may be replaced by minimized monthly aggregates connected to your account. These aggregates may include totals or averages such as calories, servings, food-group totals, classification counts, and progress measures.
  • Monthly aggregates do not retain meal photos, free-form notes, individual food names, exact meal timestamps, or AI prompts.
  • Account information, current profile settings, and monthly aggregates are retained while your account remains active and are deleted when you permanently delete the account, except where limited retention is required by law or for security.

Guest users

  • Each identifiable guest food-log record is deleted no later than 60 days after that record was created, unless the guest clears it sooner. Continued use of Sprout does not extend the retention period for an older guest record.
  • Sprout does not retain monthly history or other long-term aggregates linked to an individual guest-session identifier.
  • Sprout may retain de-identified, combined service statistics that cannot reasonably be linked back to a guest, account, or device.

Account deletion, backups, and logs

When you permanently delete your account, we delete the active account and associated user data from our active systems. Limited encrypted backups and security or diagnostic logs may remain for up to 90 days before scheduled deletion, unless longer retention is required by law, necessary to investigate abuse or security incidents, or needed to establish or defend legal claims. Data retained for these limited purposes is not used to provide recommendations, advertising, or personalization.

Anthropic's retention of API inputs and outputs is described in Section 4 and is controlled by Anthropic's commercial service terms.

8. Your Choices and Rights

  • Delete individual food-log entries.
  • Disable camera, photo-library, or notification permissions in device settings.

  • Permanently delete your account from the Account section of Sprout's settings.

We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling a request. We will respond within the period required by applicable law. You may also have the right to complain to your local data-protection authority.
